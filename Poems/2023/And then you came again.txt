January 2023

And then you came again...
Honestly, not you,
but the thoughts; the pain,
Unseen;true

The dream was great...
Honestly, it was not,
as it woke me up in a state;
an unsettling thought

I will not think of you, so I rest...
Honestly, I lied,.
There memories are arrows in my chest
I try to hide.

I love you still, always I will...
Honestly, not always,
but 'til the day I top this hill,
as nothing stays.
