The shaky lines, I drew there.
The contours formed from layer
to layer of different hues
with strokes tight and loose.

The longer they are going to stay
the more attention I can pay
to the little big details;
shadows of lips, highlights on the nails.

The painting is great, very fine
'An outcome of truth but perception of mine'.
Against my eyes, in the position same,
now, the person is also inside a frame.

Time flew, as things tend to change
completely, sometimes at some range.
The person now might not be around.
Although a bit faded, their painting has been safe and sound.
