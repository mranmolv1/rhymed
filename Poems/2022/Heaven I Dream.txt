25th April 2022

When I am tired enough I bed down.
My conciousness visits a small town.

Mysterious enough, winds there are untrue, Although, home for the losts, especially you.

Healthy and strong, you have that glow,
Not supposed to be, you were meant to be although.

Proud of me, sometimes you were rude,
Talked to me, you cooked me some food.

I could not taste, laws of the town prevail,
But I know it was something gods would hail.

Eventually, I leave, unlike I desire,
Not forever the winds run, nor stays a fire.

A divine place the town feels like, an angel you seem.
Like another egoist, is this the heaven I dream?
