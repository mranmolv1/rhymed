*Spring 2026*

For the moments he had cared,
those of joy you had shared
and his concern in your sorrow,
In the dreamy, distant tomorrow,
Remember.

When the acts ended, the curtains fell
And you still had tales left to tell
You turned to the one all eyes and ears
And how you shared your laughs and tears
Remember.

When you are free, spare a moment or two
Look back for a while at someone who
Amidst all that chaos, stood by you
Give his existence a meaning true
Remember him, will you?
