20th January 2026

There will be light
and I shall not see.
There may be some hope,
Beyond them all I shall be.

Hurries and worries,
The last bits of warmth,
My yearnings, I will let go—
Of all from this heart.

Time will play as it does
and bless me with its curse.
I shall be colder than you then,
and as cold as the universe.
