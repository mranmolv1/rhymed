I started with nothing,
Headed to the very top.
Cannot go indefinitely I know
But as long as I am, I go up.

It is hard to lift all of it
Keeping the memories along.
So, I should throw the bad ones
And then keep going on.

Now, positivity in thoughts
And feeling of serenity
Encourages me to go on
Before I rest for eternity.

I will have nothing in the end
Just like I had in the beginning,
But I will leave a trail to lead
The ones after me climbing.
