Colder this world feels,
and the time overruns.
Everyone I have lost,
has been here once.

Standing at the borders of—
life, the goners I have seen
I touch its rough surface
but past, I have never been.

I discuss within myself,
what lies behind this wall;
black abyss, white clouds,
or nothing, at all

If not nothing, I wish for hell;
I want to fall sheer.
I want to feel some warmth;
It's been cold here.
